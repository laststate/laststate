# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Billing Service a welcoming, harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level,
or role. Technical disagreement is welcome; personal attacks, intimidation, and
exclusion are not.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that strengthens the community include:

- being specific, patient, and respectful in technical discussion;
- assuming good intent while still asking for evidence;
- giving and accepting constructive feedback;
- acknowledging uncertainty, business constraints, and mistakes;
- helping new contributors find enough context to make progress;
- prioritizing the safety and privacy of payment data and customer information.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory language, sexualized
attention, threats, doxxing, sustained disruption, or publishing another
person's private information without permission.

## Scope

This code applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, community
chat, events, and public spaces where someone represents Billing Service.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through a [private
repository advisory](https://github.com/laststate/billing-service/security/advisories/new),
with `Code of Conduct` at the start of the report. Do not open a public issue
about a sensitive incident.

Maintainers will review reports promptly, protect reporter privacy as far as
possible, and may edit or remove content, issue a warning, temporarily restrict
participation, or permanently ban someone when necessary. Anyone with a conflict
of interest must recuse themselves from handling the report.
